Support Center

Welcome to TeachAtlas Support

Find help with installing TeachAtlas Pro, activating your subscription, recording guides, managing billing, and recovering access.

1. Install TeachAtlas

  1. Open the Chrome Web Store.
  2. Search for TeachAtlas.
  3. Select the TeachAtlas extension.
  4. Click Add to Chrome and confirm.
  5. Open Chrome's Extensions menu and pin TeachAtlas for convenient access.

TeachAtlas is currently awaiting publication in the Chrome Web Store. Installation will become available once it is published.

2. Activate TeachAtlas Pro

  1. Purchase a TeachAtlas Pro subscription through the official Stripe checkout.
  2. After checkout, follow the redirect to the TeachAtlas activation page.
  3. Click Activate TeachAtlas Pro.
  4. Copy the activation code and store it securely.
  5. Open the TeachAtlas Chrome extension.
  6. Paste the code into the Activation Code field and click Activate Pro.

TeachAtlas Pro requires an active subscription. Activation codes should be treated like passwords and should not be shared.

Activation Page

3. Record Your First Guide

  1. Open a supported webpage in Chrome.
  2. Open the TeachAtlas extension.
  3. Select Include screenshots if you want pictures in your guide.
  4. Click Start Recording.
  5. Perform the actions you want to document.
  6. Click Stop & Save Recording.
  7. Review and edit your generated instructions.
  8. Export your guide as HTML, Word, PDF, or plain text.

Some websites and protected content cannot be recorded or screenshotted because of browser restrictions and privacy safeguards.

4. Lost Your Activation Code?

If you changed computers, reinstalled Chrome, or lost your activation code, you may request a replacement without purchasing another subscription.

  1. Contact TeachAtlas Support using the email address associated with your purchase.
  2. Include the subject: TeachAtlas Activation Recovery.
  3. Explain that you need a replacement code.
  4. Support will provide instructions for verifying ownership of the subscription.
  5. Once ownership and subscription eligibility are confirmed, a replacement code can be issued.

Never email your password, payment card information, or existing activation code. Replacement codes may invalidate older codes.

Request Activation Recovery

5. Manage Your Subscription

TeachAtlas Pro is billed at $9 USD per month, excluding any applicable taxes.

For billing changes, payment methods, receipts, or subscription cancellation, use the customer billing management link supplied with your Stripe checkout or billing email.

If you cannot find your billing management link, contact TeachAtlas Support.

Canceling a subscription may end access immediately or at the end of the paid billing period, depending on the cancellation settings.

6. Frequently Asked Questions

Are my guides stored online?

TeachAtlas stores your saved guides locally in your browser. Use the backup feature to preserve copies of your work.

Can I use TeachAtlas on multiple websites?

TeachAtlas supports recording across compatible websites and browser tabs, subject to site access and browser restrictions.

Why didn't a screenshot appear?

Screenshots are optional. Some screenshots may be skipped because of protected content, privacy protections, or browser limitations.

What if my subscription expires?

TeachAtlas Pro checks subscription eligibility through its license server. Pro functionality requires a qualifying subscription.
