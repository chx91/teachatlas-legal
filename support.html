Support Center

Quick help for installing, recording, exporting and using TeachAtlas Pro.

Updated for 2.5.18

Install TeachAtlas

Chrome Web Store ↗ Firefox Add-ons ↗ Edge Add-ons ↗

On Edge, search for “TeachAtlas” in the store. After installing, open TeachAtlas from your browser’s Extensions menu. Supported sites may ask you to grant access.

Record and export

  1. Open a website and select Start Recording in TeachAtlas. Grant website access if prompted.
  2. Complete your task. Use Pause Recording when needed; screenshots are optional.
  3. Choose Stop & Save Recording, then Open My Guides to review and edit.
  4. Check the text and screenshots for private information, then export in an available format.

Screenshots are not automatically redacted. Use the privacy-cover tools or remove sensitive images before exporting or publishing. Protected webpages and fast navigation can prevent some captures.

Free and Pro at a glance

Free — $0

One saved recording at a time, up to 40 steps and 20 screenshots. Export to TXT or PDF.

Recording pauses at the Free step limit.

Pro — $9/month

More recordings and screenshots (subject to browser limits), HTML, Word and Markdown exports, manual building, backups and Cloud Share links, where supported.

Pro activation and billing

After subscribing, follow the checkout link to the activation page to obtain your code. In the extension, open Settings and Pro Activation, enter the code and activate Pro. Keep your code private.

Activation page ↗ Manage or cancel billing ↗

Uninstalling TeachAtlas does not cancel a Stripe subscription. For activation-code recovery, contact support using the email associated with your purchase. Do not email your activation code or payment details.

Cloud Share links

Open a saved recording or manual and choose its Share option. Review the content, choose the offered download formats and publish a public link. You can copy, update or revoke that link.

Anyone with the link can view the published content without TeachAtlas. Download restrictions do not stop copying, and revocation cannot retrieve copies already saved by others.

Quick fixes

Recording won’t start?

Try a normal website, not a browser settings or internal page. Check the website access prompt, then reopen TeachAtlas and try again. Some pages cannot be recorded.

A screenshot is missing?

Check that Include screenshots was selected. Browser restrictions, navigation speed and site permissions may prevent individual images from being captured.

How do I change the interface language?

Use the language control in TeachAtlas to select a language or automatic website-language detection. This changes the extension interface, not your recorded text.

Need a replacement activation code or a public link removed?

Contact support with your purchase email or the affected public link and a short explanation. Support may need to verify subscription ownership. Never send a password, payment card number or activation code.

Still need help?

Email us about installation, billing, activation, or a privacy issue. Describe the problem and include your browser and TeachAtlas version when useful.
