Straightforward workflow

Record. Review. Export.

  1. Record: start a guide and complete a supported browser task; choose whether to capture screenshots.
  2. Review: revise step wording, reorder or edit instructions, and remove anything that should not appear in the guide.
  3. Export: download a document for local distribution. Optional Pro features support additional formats and Cloud Share links.

Use approved test data when recording. Staff should review screenshots and text for confidential, personal, controlled or sensitive information before exporting or sharing. Some sites and browser pages cannot be recorded.

IT and procurement review

A clear path for your technology review.

TeachAtlas is a browser extension for Chrome, Firefox and Microsoft Edge. Guide recording and editing are browser-based; Pro offers optional published Cloud Share links. Share only material your organization has approved for external hosting.

  • Privacy: review the privacy policy for local storage, licensing and optional cloud features.
  • Website access: recording may require browser site permissions; verify that the extension can be deployed under your managed-browser policies.
  • Cloud features: request an IT/security review before using external sharing with agency content. Local file export may be more appropriate for some workflows.
  • Compliance: request any accessibility, security and procurement documentation you require. Do not assume federal authorization, FedRAMP approval or formal accessibility conformance without written confirmation.

TeachAtlas is not represented as an approved or certified government product. Agencies must independently determine whether it meets their policies and applicable requirements.

Common questions

Before your department subscribes

Can a department buy 10, 25 or 50 licenses?

Please contact TeachAtlas with your expected user count. The current public purchase flow is for individual Pro subscriptions; a centralized multi-license portal is not yet offered.

Can an agency buy an annual subscription by purchase order?

Standard Pro is currently billed monthly through Stripe. Contact us to discuss invoicing, annual terms or purchase-order requirements before initiating procurement. Acceptance of those terms is not guaranteed.

Does TeachAtlas work with sensitive agency information?

Do not capture or publish sensitive material unless your organization's IT and privacy teams approve the workflow. Use test data and review recordings before export. Cloud Share is optional and needs separate review.

Can staff try it before purchasing?

Yes. The Free version allows one saved guide at a time, up to 40 steps and 20 screenshots, with TXT and PDF export.
