Privacy Policy

1. Overview

TeachAtlas is designed to help users document workflows while keeping recorded guides on their own devices. This policy describes the data handled by the TeachAtlas browser extension.

2. Information the Extension Processes

When recording is enabled, TeachAtlas may process browser interactions, including clicked controls, action descriptions, click positions, navigation events, and optional screenshots of visible web pages.

Screenshots may contain visible website content, including information displayed on the page. Users should review captured materials before saving, exporting, or sharing them.

3. Local Storage

The current TeachAtlas extension stores recorded guides and related information locally within the user's browser profile.

Screenshots are stored locally using browser storage technologies, including IndexedDB. TeachAtlas does not currently provide a cloud synchronization or hosted guide-storage service.

4. Website Access and Permissions

TeachAtlas requires certain browser permissions to record actions and optionally capture screenshots. Some website access must be enabled by the user.

Website access is used to provide the requested recording functionality. Certain browser-protected pages cannot be recorded.

5. Sensitive Information

TeachAtlas does not intentionally record values entered into password fields or other text-entry fields as step descriptions.

The extension includes safeguards intended to prevent screenshots on pages containing certain sensitive form elements, entered information, embedded content, or other potentially private areas.

These safeguards are not guaranteed to identify every type of sensitive information. Users should avoid recording confidential, financial, medical, or otherwise private information and should inspect guides before sharing.

6. Data Sharing

The current extension does not automatically upload recorded guides or screenshots to a TeachAtlas server. Users may choose to export their guides and share those files with others.

Users are responsible for deciding where exported files are stored and with whom they are shared.

7. Payments

Paid subscriptions may be offered through Stripe. When paid subscriptions become available, payment and billing information will be processed through Stripe's hosted payment services rather than being entered directly into the TeachAtlas extension.

Stripe's handling of payment information is subject to its own privacy policies.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally saved guides remain in the user's browser storage until they are deleted or that storage is cleared. TeachAtlas provides guide-management and deletion features.

Exported copies and backups are separate files. Deleting a guide from the extension does not automatically delete copies stored elsewhere.

9. Children's Privacy

TeachAtlas is intended for educators, professionals, and other users who can lawfully use the service. It is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated when TeachAtlas changes its features, data practices, or services. The latest version will be posted on this page.
