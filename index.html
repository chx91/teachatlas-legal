2. Recording, screenshots, and your privacy choices

TeachAtlas records supported browser actions to create editable guides in supported versions of Chrome, Firefox, and Edge. Recording is controlled by the user, and screenshots are optional. Supported versions also allow user-initiated manual screenshot capture, including while recording is paused, and Pro manual guide creation and editing. When screenshots are enabled and any applicable browser access or privacy confirmation is granted, TeachAtlas may capture visible webpage content as it appears. Screenshots can incidentally include names, messages, text entered into fields, account details, financial or health information, or confidential records, even though TeachAtlas does not intentionally copy typed field values into guide step descriptions. Screenshots are not automatically masked or redacted; users must review and cover or remove private details themselves. Browser security restrictions and technical limits can prevent capture on some pages. Page addresses, visible labels, navigation details and screenshots may themselves reveal personal information or confidential identifiers. Do not use TeachAtlas on private/incognito browsing windows, restricted workspaces or sensitive records unless the relevant use is permitted; do not enable private-window access.

You are responsible for what you capture, export, or share. Before recording, check what is visible and close or hide sensitive content, use fictional data, or leave screenshots off. Record and share content only when you have the necessary permission.

Before exporting or publishing, review all guide or manual text, chapter headings, and every screenshot. Use Hide private details to cover sensitive areas, or remove any screenshot you cannot safely share. Check the final export before distributing it. The editing tools and review prompts assist you with this review. They cannot guarantee that every private detail has been removed. Local originals and backups may still contain the original images, so keep those files private.

3. Local storage, exports, and backups

Saved guides and screenshots are kept locally in the browser profile where TeachAtlas is installed, using browser storage that can include extension storage and IndexedDB. A guide stored in Chrome is not automatically synchronized to Firefox or Edge, or vice versa. TeachAtlas does not currently offer hosted cloud synchronization of guide libraries. The extension does not automatically upload recorded guide content or screenshots during ordinary recording or license verification. Cloud publishing is a separate, optional Pro action, not automatic library synchronization.

When you export, print, download, or share a guide or manual, copies may exist outside the extension. Available formats depend on the installed version and plan and may include TXT, PDF, HTML, Word, and Markdown. Exports and backups may contain personal information; their content and distribution remain your responsibility. Deleting a local guide or changing a manual does not itself remove its separately published cloud copy or any files, browser downloads, backups, or copies kept by others. Browser cleanup, profile deletion, device failure, or uninstalling may remove local data; keep backups of important work.

4. Optional public cloud sharing (Pro)

When you choose to publish or update a guide or manual and affirmatively confirm that you have reviewed its contents, TeachAtlas transmits the selected title, chapter headings and instructions in their selected order (for manuals), step descriptions, optional screenshots, click-highlight positions, and permitted download formats to the Cloudflare-hosted TeachAtlas sharing service over HTTPS. Cloudflare stores the published data and creates a publicly accessible URL that anyone with the link may open. This is not password-protected or restricted to named recipients. People with the link do not need a TeachAtlas account. Links may be forwarded or become discoverable through recipients or other websites. Treat all published material as public, not as a private or access-controlled document. Cloud Share is intended for non-sensitive instructional content, preferably using fictional or anonymized examples. Review each title, instruction, and rendered image before publishing; copying or forwarding a public link can expose its contents to unintended recipients. Do not publish passwords, authentication credentials, financial or payment information, health information, private communications, children's personal information, private records, confidential business data, or other sensitive material. Use synthetic or redacted examples instead. Remove personal information about other people before publishing unless you have lawful authority and all required consents, including express written consent where required by the applicable browser store's policies. For Microsoft Edge, do not use Cloud Share for personal information covered by Microsoft Edge Add-ons developer policy section 1.5.3 unless the disclosure is expressly permitted under that policy. Where publication of a non-user's personal information is otherwise permitted, obtain their express written consent and allow them to withdraw that consent at any time. Consent does not override any applicable restriction on disclosure. Anyone who can view public content may copy, screenshot, print, save, or redistribute it, even if particular download buttons are disabled. Download settings are not a guarantee against copying or redistribution.

You can update an existing publication at the same address or revoke its link while authorized by the service. A manual with a different title or chapter selection/order may be treated as a separate publication with a separate link. Revoking one publication does not revoke any other publication that contains the same content. Revocation removes access to the hosted copy, but does not recall information others have already accessed, photographed, printed, or downloaded. Published guides and manuals are not intended as private storage or a backup service. Currently there is no automatic public-link expiration; contact support if you cannot revoke a publication after losing Pro access or clearing local browser data. If your personal information appears in a publication without appropriate permission, contact support to request review or removal. Guide and manual content is not required by the licensing service to validate Pro status.

5. Permissions and website access

In Chrome and Edge, TeachAtlas uses browser extension capabilities such as local storage, access to the active webpage, scripting on supported pages, and a side panel to perform user-requested recording, editing, and optional screenshot capture. In Firefox, TeachAtlas uses supported Firefox extension and sidebar capabilities and may separately request permission for website access. For optional Pro Cloud Share in Firefox, the browser may also request consent before website content or activity information is transmitted. Users can decline optional access or sharing; some related features may then be unavailable. You can stop recording, turn screenshots off, and remove granted website access in your browser's extension settings. In Firefox, you can also manage optional data transmission permissions in the add-on settings. Revoking recording or data-transmission permission does not automatically remove previously published copies. The exact permission prompts, features, and supported websites vary by browser and extension version. Browser-internal, protected, or restricted pages may be unavailable.

Browser activity, page addresses, labels, and visible website content are processed for the user-requested guide-building function, not to monitor browsing continuously or create a browsing-history advertising profile. TeachAtlas does not send a user's recorded guide library to its licensing service.

When automatic interface language detection is enabled, TeachAtlas may inspect the declared language of an accessible website to select a supported interface translation. It stores the detected language and your selection locally. You can override automatic detection by choosing a language from the globe menu. The localization feature does not automatically translate recorded instructions, user-created titles, or website content; it does not use a remote translation service.

6. Pro activation and service providers

Purchases and subscription management use Stripe. Activation and entitlement checks, and optional Pro guide and manual publishing, communicate with TeachAtlas services hosted on Cloudflare. TeachAtlas web pages are hosted through GitHub Pages. Those providers may process technical, transactional, or account data needed to perform their services under their respective policies. This includes IP addresses and request information when visiting a website or a public share link. The activation page sends the checkout session reference to the licensing service when you request activation. Protect the checkout link and activation code like credentials; do not include them in screenshots or support emails.

When the extension checks whether a Pro subscription is eligible, its activation credential is sent in an HTTPS authorization header to the licensing service. That credential is also sent to the separate sharing service to authorize publishing, updating, or revoking a public link. It is not included in the public guide or manual payload. Your recorded guides and screenshots are not required for routine license verification. When you publish, the content payload consists of the title, ordered instruction text, rendered screenshot images and optional click-marker coordinates, and selected download formats; a full browsing history or guide library is not part of that payload. Instructions or screenshots may nevertheless reveal page addresses or personal information.

Links to browser stores, payment services and other websites are subject to their own policies. Website referral URLs may identify the referring TeachAtlas page; they are not part of recorded guides.
