2. Recording and screenshot safeguards

Recording begins when you use the extension's recording controls on a supported website. The extension is intended to capture actions and the context needed for a guide, not what you type into password or other text-entry fields as step descriptions. Screenshot capture is controlled by available settings and restrictions in the installed version.

Visible screenshots can nonetheless contain personal or confidential information displayed on a webpage. TeachAtlas applies safeguards intended to skip some sensitive content, but no automated safeguard can identify everything. You are responsible for checking and, where needed, removing or redacting information before exporting or sharing.

3. Local storage, exports, and backups

Saved guides and screenshots are kept locally using browser storage, which can include extension storage and IndexedDB. TeachAtlas does not currently offer hosted cloud synchronization of guide libraries. The extension does not automatically send your recorded guide content or screenshots to a TeachAtlas server as part of ordinary recording and licensing.

When you export, print, download, or share a guide, that copy is handled outside the extension. Deleting a guide in TeachAtlas does not erase exported files, browser downloads, your backups, or copies shared with other people. Browser cleanup, profile deletion, device failure, or uninstalling may remove local data; keep backups of important work.

4. Permissions and website access

TeachAtlas may ask for access to supported webpages or browser capabilities to recognize interactions, capture optional screenshots, save guides, and perform user-requested functions. Permission prompts and supported websites vary by browser and extension version. Browser-internal, protected, or restricted pages may be unavailable.

Browser activity and page information are processed for the user-requested guide-building function, not to create a browsing-history advertising profile.

5. Pro activation and service providers

Purchases and subscription management use Stripe. Activation and entitlement checks communicate with a TeachAtlas licensing service hosted on Cloudflare. TeachAtlas web pages are hosted through GitHub Pages. Those providers may process technical, transactional, or account data needed to perform their services under their respective policies.

When the extension checks whether a Pro subscription is eligible, the license credential and necessary validation information are transmitted securely to the licensing service. Your recorded guides and screenshots are not required for this check.

6. Selling, advertising, and disclosure

TeachAtlas does not sell your recorded guide content or screenshots, and does not use them for targeted advertising. We do not provide recorded guides to advertisers. We may disclose information to the service providers described above when needed to deliver the service, respond to lawful process, investigate security incidents, or protect users and the service in accordance with applicable law.

Use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Retention and deletion

Guide content remains in browser storage until you delete it, clear that browser storage, or otherwise remove the relevant profile or extension data. TeachAtlas does not remotely erase independently exported copies.

Subscription, license, transaction, and support records are retained as needed to provide paid access, address disputes or fraud, keep required accounting records, or meet applicable legal obligations. We do not promise that canceling a subscription immediately deletes transaction or license records.
