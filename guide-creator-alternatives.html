SOP software, explained

How to create a step-by-step SOP

A standard operating procedure (SOP) is a repeatable set of instructions. The clearest SOPs show what to do, in what order, and what successful completion looks like.

1

Perform the task

Start recording, then complete the browser process exactly as someone else should do it. Avoid capturing private data.

2

Review the steps

Fix unclear instructions, remove unnecessary actions, and annotate screenshots where visual detail helps.

3

Deliver the guide

Export a PDF or text guide. With TeachAtlas Pro, you can alternatively share a public link and revoke it later.

For complex business operations, also add an owner, review date, prerequisites, and an exception-handling section to the SOP.

Questions people ask

Before choosing a Scribe or Tango alternative

Is there a free Scribe alternative for browser workflows?

Yes. TeachAtlas includes a free Chrome and Firefox extension with limited saved guides, steps and screenshots. Mimik also offers a browser-focused approach, while Scribe itself has a free browser plan. Compare limits before deciding.

Does TeachAtlas record desktop software?

No. TeachAtlas is focused on supported browser actions. If you need to document programs outside the browser, consider desktop-capable tools such as Folge or eligible paid plans from Scribe or Tango.

Can TeachAtlas make standard operating procedures?

Yes. It can turn browser workflows into step-by-step instructions with optional screenshots. Review and adjust the generated steps before using a guide as an official SOP, and add your organization's required context.

How is TeachAtlas different from a screen recorder?

Traditional screen recorders primarily create videos. TeachAtlas records browser actions as editable written steps with optional screenshots, intended for printable or shareable instructions.

Can people read a guide without installing TeachAtlas?

Yes, with TeachAtlas Pro's optional Cloud Share feature. A published public link can be opened by people who do not have the extension. Anyone with the link may view the published content; you can update or revoke it.
