Understand the category

What does process documentation software do?

It helps people capture, standardize, update and distribute instructions for repeatable work. Depending on the tool, output may be a written procedure, numbered screenshots, a video, an interactive walkthrough or a central knowledge base.

Selection checklist

Six questions before you choose an SOP creator

  1. What are you documenting?

    Is the task in a browser, native desktop application, on paper or in a physical workspace? A browser extension is not a full desktop recorder.

  2. Who consumes the instructions?

    Single users may need a PDF. Teams may need a searchable hub, review workflow, permissions and change logs.

  3. Which output formats matter?

    Check whether your preferred edition provides text, HTML, PDF, Word, screenshots, video or interactive guidance. Do not assume every format is offered on every plan.

  4. How sensitive is the workflow?

    Check local storage, screenshot redaction needs, sharing permissions, data retention and security review. A shareable URL should never contain secrets.

  5. Who updates documents?

    Establish version owners, review dates, approval rules and a process for retiring obsolete guidance.

  6. What is the realistic total cost?

    Consider per-user charges, training time, editing effort, publishing features and any limits on free plans. Confirm current pricing directly with each provider.

What to document

Three high-value SOP examples

Customer support handoff

Document where tickets arrive, priority rules, required context, assignment steps and when to escalate.

Weekly reporting

Define the date range, source of truth, verification checks, report recipient and confirmation of delivery.

New team member setup

Document approved access requests, training links, task order and a checklist for confirming setup.

Tip: Good process documentation connects the action to a verification step. "Select Save" is not enough if the system might reject the change.

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between SOP software and a guide recorder?

A guide recorder focuses on documenting actions as they are performed. A full SOP also establishes purpose, responsibilities, review cycles, exceptions and governance. You can use both together.

Can I document a process for free?

Yes. Start with an editable SOP template. For compatible browser tasks, TeachAtlas also offers a Free version, subject to its current plan limitations.

Should I choose software by its search ranking?

No. Compare the exact workflow, security requirements, available exports, collaboration features and current price before selecting a tool.
