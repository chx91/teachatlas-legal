Good to know

Common questions

Can TeachAtlas record every website?

No. You control when recording starts. Protected pages, permissions and technical restrictions can prevent recording or screenshot capture.

Where are my instructions stored?

Saved instructions and screenshots normally stay locally in your browser. They do not automatically sync across browsers. Pro Cloud Share is an optional publishing action.

Can I cancel Pro?

Yes, using the Stripe billing portal. Uninstalling the extension does not cancel your subscription. See the billing terms.
