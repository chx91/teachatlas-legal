Good to know

A few practical answers.

What does TeachAtlas actually record?

TeachAtlas records supported browser clicks and navigation after you start recording. Optional screenshots can add visual context. It is not a continuous video or unrestricted browsing-history recorder, and browser-protected pages may be unavailable.

Is TeachAtlas free to use?

Yes. The Free Version lets you save one recording at a time, with up to 40 steps and 20 screenshots per recording, and export as TXT or PDF. Pro is optional and costs $9 USD/month plus applicable taxes.

Can I share instructions without installing TeachAtlas?

Pro users can intentionally publish reviewed instructions or manuals through Cloud Share. Anyone who has the public link can view them without a TeachAtlas account. These links are not private and can be forwarded.

Where are my recordings stored?

Ordinary saved recordings and screenshots are kept locally in your browser. They do not automatically synchronize across browsers. Public Cloud Share copies are hosted separately, only when intentionally published.

Can I use it on any webpage?

No. Browser-internal and protected webpages, website permissions, or technical restrictions can prevent some actions and screenshot captures. Features also vary by browser version.

Can I cancel Pro?

Yes. Manage or cancel your subscription through the Stripe billing portal. Uninstalling the extension does not cancel recurring billing. See the subscription terms for details.
