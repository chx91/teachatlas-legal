Frequently Asked Questions

Questions? We have answers.

What exactly does TeachAtlas do?

TeachAtlas is a browser extension that records supported browser actions and turns them into editable, step-by-step guides.

Do I need technical experience?

No programming experience is required to use TeachAtlas. You record a task, review its instructions, and export the finished guide.

Can I record every website?

Not every page can be recorded. Browser security restrictions, protected pages, and privacy safeguards may prevent certain actions or screenshots from being captured.

Where are my guides stored?

Saved guides are stored locally in your browser. We recommend using the backup feature to preserve important work.

How much does TeachAtlas Pro cost?

TeachAtlas Pro costs $9 USD per month, excluding any applicable taxes. It requires an active subscription to use Pro features.

How do I activate my subscription?

After checkout, the TeachAtlas activation page verifies your purchase and issues an activation code. Enter the code in the Chrome extension to unlock Pro.

What if I lose my activation code?

Contact our Support Center. After ownership and subscription eligibility are verified, a replacement code can be issued.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes. Subscription cancellation and billing management are handled through Stripe or with support assistance. The billing portal shows the cancellation effective date. Uninstalling the extension does not cancel billing.
